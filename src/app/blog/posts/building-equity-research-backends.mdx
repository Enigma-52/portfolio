At FrontPage, one of my core responsibilities was building a backend platform that could serve financial data,
concall artifacts, and AI-generated insights in a single product experience.

## What we optimized for

- Predictable API behavior under changing data volume.
- Fast retrieval for user-facing research views.
- Clear separation between ingestion pipelines and serving workloads.

## Architecture decisions that helped

- **TypeScript + Node.js services** for fast feature iteration with strong types.
- **PostgreSQL** as the source of truth for structured financial entities.
- **Redis** for low-latency reads on hot paths.
- A strict separation of ingestion jobs from user-facing request flows.

## Practical takeaway

The biggest win was not a single framework choice, but disciplined boundaries between data ingestion,
transformation, and serving. That kept the product stable while we shipped quickly.
