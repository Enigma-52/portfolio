At Zelthy, my current work focuses on core backend modules for a multi-tenant SaaS product built on Python and Zango.

## Constraints that shape decisions

- Every change must behave safely across tenant boundaries.
- Incremental delivery is preferred over large risky rewrites.
- Reliability in production matters more than idealized architecture.

## Working approach

- Ship in small, verifiable increments.
- Keep contracts explicit at module boundaries.
- Add tests around regressions before extending behavior.
- Prioritize stable production behavior over speculative abstractions.

## Practical takeaway

Crisp execution in multi-tenant systems comes from consistency: small releases, clear contracts, and reliability-first engineering.
